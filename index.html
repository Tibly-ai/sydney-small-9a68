The Live Show

A set built on old standards and a few of her own.

Sydney fronts a rotating trio of New York's sharpest session players. Expect low light, a full room, and songs that stretch out when the night calls for it. No two sets run the same.

Private Events

Weddings, galas, and rooms that need a little smoke and swing.

Sydney has sung first dances, corporate holiday parties, and quiet backyard dinners for two hundred guests. She'll build the set list around your night, from cocktail hour to the last slow number.

Original Music

Two records, one more on the way.

Written and recorded in a fourth floor walk-up in Washington Heights. Streaming everywhere, played loudest at the shows where they were born.
