What a booking looks like

Every show is built to fit the room.

Sydney Small Music has played rooftop cocktail hours, hotel ballrooms, small Brooklyn listening rooms, and backyard receptions upstate. Sydney sings jazz standards, soul, and a few well-placed contemporary covers, backed by a trio, quartet, or solo with piano, depending on your space and budget.

We work directly with planners and hosts, no agency markup, no middleman. Fill out the form below with your date, venue, and what you're hoping to spend. Sydney reads every inquiry personally and replies within two business days with a set list, a band size, and a real quote.
